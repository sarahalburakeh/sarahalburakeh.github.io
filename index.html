Prior work

Earlier career experiences shaped my approach to content planning and creation, big-picture thinking, and a consistent ability to simplify complexity without losing intent. Highlights include:

🥋 Chinkilla · Co-founder

Brand development · Strategic risk-taking · Community growth · Public speaking

Co-founding Chinkilla has been one of my most meaningful long-term projects. What began as a website outlined in a cafe evolved into an international platform supporting women in martial arts across Europe. I helped build it from the ground up: pitching to investors, speaking on live TV (in German no less), and delivering a TEDx talk to a live audience. Organizing a bootcamp in the Austrian mountains was a high-risk decision that became one of our strongest revenue streams, going from barely breaking even to now hosting UFC fighters. Working with designers on clothing lines, managing paid marketing campaigns, and overseeing manufacturing taught me adaptability and end-to-end brand development.

🧬 Inspire · Healthcare Content & Strategy

Content strategy · Complex-to-simple translation · Sponsored content management · Accuracy & empathy

I entered the healthcare space knowing there would be a steep learning curve. As a content consultant at Inspire, I supported pharmaceutical brands in refining drug-treatment articles and quickly recognized the challenge of translating complex concepts—such as mechanisms of action—into content suitable for general audiences. I later moved into the sponsored content manager role, where accuracy and empathy became equally essential. This was my first strategic experience shaping content that balances technical detail with accessibility.

💻 Blue Kangaroo · Startup Foundations

Startup agility · Cross-functional collaboration · Technical documentation · Problem-solving

My first ever editorial role was at a Silicon Valley startup called BlueRoo.ai (later Blue Kangaroo). Like many startups, it didn’t survive, but the eight years I spent there were foundational. Beyond writing website content, I developed business requirement documentation for the CMS, created technical documentation, and edited video scripts. I also worked in SQL to generate reports, supported the QA team on product testing, and collaborated with data scientists on early chatbot implementations—long before generative AI. Working with brands such as Home Depot, American Eagle, and McAfee taught me to deliver in high-pressure, resource-constrained environments.
